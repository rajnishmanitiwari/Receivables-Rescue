Prototype · synthetic data · deterministic rules · sends nothing

What should happen to each overdue invoice today?

Move the date. The decision for each invoice is computed by a fixed rule function from its history, not by a model. Disputes and opt-outs stay paused until a person resolves them.

Tier thresholds are placeholders. The reply labels shown were set by the scenario; the keyword classifier behind them is a basic English-only baseline. Invoices and customers are fictional. Not validated on real invoices or collections.
